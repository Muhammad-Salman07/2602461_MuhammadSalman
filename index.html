Most Expensive Vehicles to Steal and Sell in GTA 6

Grand Theft Auto 6 arrives on November 19, 2026, for PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X and S. Stealing cars in Leonida works very differently from the old games.
A parked car can sit behind layers of security, with a tracker inside and a key cloner needed to start it. The car next to it opens with a slim jim in seconds. The car worth the most money on paper is often the most difficult car to steal on the street.

How does car theft work in GTA 6?

Rockstar has split parked cars into security tiers. The tier decides almost everything about a theft. Older cars sit at the bottom, with simple locks and no electronics to fight. Modern luxury cars and supercars sit at the top, with alarms, electronic locks, and trackers. You unlock the better theft tools as the story moves on. You unlock the fences who can strip those trackers in the same way. For example, when Jason walks up to a red classic car in a parking lot, the game offers two choices on screen: Slim Jim or Smash Window. One way is quiet and takes a timed minigame with the slim jim. The other way is fast and loud, and a loud break-in on a car with security brings police attention.
